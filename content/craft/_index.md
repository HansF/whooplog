Two airframes share this site. Most pages apply to both, because the switch map, rates and
throttle curve are pilot settings rather than craft settings. Pages that describe a specific
frame, its motors or its tune are marked with that craft only.

- **Crafty** — the original 1S whoop.
  [Build spec](/reference/build-betafpvg473-v2-1s/).
- **Air65** — a BetaFPV Air65 added in September 2026.
  [Build spec](/reference/build-air65/).

Both run the same `BETAFPVG473_V2` board target, so the board name does not distinguish them.
Their MCU IDs and their gyros do.
